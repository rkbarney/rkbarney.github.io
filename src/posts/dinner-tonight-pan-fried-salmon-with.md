Overall the flavors worked well but texturally the puree, cabbage, and velouté were a bit too similar. I might like a little more heat (maybe a half-tablespoon of gochujang?). Additionally sweet potato with miso was a touch over sweet. If I were to do it again from scratch, some kind of crispy regular potato or plain rice would be a better starch for this dish. Something more toothsome like broccolini would be a better vegetable pairing. Also I forgot that I broiled shishito peppers from a friends garden… I should check on those.</p><p>Either way here’s a recipe.</p><h3><strong>Ingredients:</strong></h3><ul><li><p>.5 lb salmon fillets </p></li><li><p>.5 cabbage (I grew I my garden!)</p></li><li><p>3 sweet potatoes (thanks </p></li><li><p>butter </p></li><li><p>Heavy cream</p></li><li><p>Miso paste</p></li><li><p>Vegetable or fish stock</p></li><li><p>2 cloves garlic</p></li><li><p>.5 small jalapeño peppers</p></li><li><p></p></li></ul><h3><strong>Method</strong></h3><p>0. <strong>Bake or boil the sweet potatoes</strong> until they pierce easily with a fork (45 min).</p><p>1. <strong>Braise cabbage.</strong> Cut cabbage into wedges and fry in vegetable oil in medium hot pan. Once golden brown, cover in stock and simmer on low with a lid.</p><p>2. <strong>Prep aromatics.</strong> Dice two cloves garlic and the jalapeño roughly (these will be strained out later)</p><p>3. <strong>Cook the salmon.</strong> Heat 2 tablespoons of butter plus neutral cooking oil in a nonstick pan. With a paper towel pat down and dry salmon fillets. Dust all sides evenly with salt. Fry skin side down in the oil until skin is crispy. Flip and cook for 1 more minute in the flesh side. Remove the fish from the pan, drain used oil. </p><p>4. <strong>Make the velouté.</strong> Reduce heat to medium. Add 3 tablespoons of butter to the pan plus the garlic and jalapeño, cook until fragrant and then add 3 tablespoons of all-purpose flour and whisk into a roux. Once the roux is thickened and begins to brown, add in 1-2 cups of the stock (that the cabbage has been cooking in) and reduce until thickened. Turn down the heat to low and slowly whisk in heavy cream until it’s creamy smooth and you can swipe your finger across the back of the spatula add in 2 tablespoons of miso paste and whisk this in until incorporated and smooth. Season to taste with salt and pepper and keep warm until ready to serve. Before serving, strain into a Pyrex to remove clumps and aromatics.</p><p>5. <strong>Make the sweet potato puree.</strong> Remove sweet potato from the skins and put into a blender with 2 tablespoons of butter, a few tablespoons of the velouté and a half cup of heavy cream. Blend until smooth, adding stock or cream as needed.</p><p>6. Plate. Start by plating the puree, with cabbage on top. Next pour in desired amount of velouté, place salmon skin side up on top of velouté, garnish with diced parsley, chive, or spring onions.</p><p></p><p>Let me know if you like these kinds of posts!</p>
