Hi. I'm Richard Barney. I'm a data scientist and stand-up comedian based in San Francisco.

I've been writing on Substack for a while under the name [Leftovers, CA](https://richardbarney.substack.com). It's been good. But I've been thinking about the indie web lately — the idea that you should own your stuff, that platforms come and go, and that your personal site is the one place on the internet that's actually yours.

So now I'm posting here first. The same writing will go to Substack too (where you can subscribe to get it in your inbox), but this is the canonical home.

## What I'll write about

Mostly the same stuff as Leftovers, CA: living in California, comedy, data, whatever's on my mind.

## How to follow along

The best way is RSS — grab [the feed](/feed.xml) and drop it in your reader. I use [justrss.app](https://justrss.app) (which I built). Or subscribe on [Substack](https://richardbarney.substack.com) if you prefer email.

Good to have you here.
