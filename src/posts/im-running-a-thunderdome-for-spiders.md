during his tenure in the windowsill:</p><ol><li><p>“I bet watching spiders was Netflix for cave men.”</p></li><li><p>“He’s playing an important role in the ecosystem (of my house).”</p></li><li><p>“The universe is just stuff. I am one arrangement of stuff, the spider is another. Therefore, I and the spider are co-equal parts of an infinite universe.”</p></li></ol><p>I saw him each day that I showered. I’d say “Whatsup”.</p><p>Google says that spiders can live up to two years and our platonic interspecies relationship might have lasted that long but after a few months I became involved in a same-species romantic relationship. Something had to be done for a few reasons: </p><ol><li><p>She did not like the spider in the shower,</p></li><li><p>I liked her more than I liked the spider, and</p></li><li><p>I am not loyal to my homies.</p></li></ol><p>Friend breakups are hard but I wanted to be cool about it so I tried to end things with with a get-him-to-run-onto-junk-mail-and-release-outside method. Turns out I have about three humane-capture attempts in me before I become frustrated and start muttering shit like “I’m gonna fucking kill you”. But, the spider kept ducking into a crack quicker than I could get him.</p><p>That’s when I came up with the Dirt Devil Solution: slowly inch the Dirt Devil vacuum toward the spider so he doesn’t bolt There’s no way around it: whoever is left inside is fucking boss.</p><p>It’s probably something like that scene in Gladiator where Russel Crowe straight up merks a bunch of guys, decapitates one,
