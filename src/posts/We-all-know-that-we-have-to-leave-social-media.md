# We all know that we have to leave social media.

But right now we don’t know where to go but actually I think we do.

We all need to make our own personal websites.

I’m serious. Hang with me. I know you don’t want to. I know you learned the absolute bare minimum of html to embed a Taking Back Sunday music video onto your MySpace in 2003 but I promise it’s not going to be like that this time. So hear me out.

And look I didn’t want to have to do this either. I’m just like you.

I spent decades avoiding it.

When MySpace started sucking I left for Facebook. When Facebook started sucking I left for Instagram and Twitter. When Twitter became X and Instagram became an absolute dogshit hellsite I moved to Threads.

And Threads fucking sucks. It sucked on day 1 and it sucked on day today and it will suck on day forever because Mark Zuckerberg is a bastard and even if he wasn’t there it’s a publicly traded company and the shareholders demand infinite growth.

So I’m writing this on Substack. You’re probably reading this on Substack (if you’re reading it at all). There’s a good chance you like Substack. That’s fine. You don’t need to leave… yet. (To be fair, there’s a chance that Substack won't become entirely en-dogshittified but I’m not going to hold my breath. (Footnote: On one hand they don’t have public shareholders demanding infinite profits and they’ve made some potentially sustainable decisions about monetization, ability to take your property and subscribers with you, etc.)

But, Substack is still a privately-owned, for-profit company. They farm engagement with push notifications and little annoying bubbles and your homepage is an algorithmic news feed bumps images, short form vertical video and occasionally really bizarre scary things that I’m like WTF WHY IS THIS RECOMMENDED TO ME THIS DUDE IS A FREAK WITH BAD IDEAS… (I’ll just interact with it a little to see what’s going on).

So yeah, Substack is almost definitely gonna suck soon if it doesn’t already and then what are we going to do? Move to another platform owned by a bunch of other (or maybe the same) weird evil rich guys? Will we ever learn not to trust weird evil rich guys with our attention, our art, with the very substance of our lives in photos and videos?

“Charlie Brown wake up!” I scream at the TV, “Weird evil guys will never let you kick the football. They profit from your misery!”
